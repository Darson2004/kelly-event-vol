# H7 — behavioral-attention: ratio_last_rm (+) — recency anchoring after a quiet print

**Cell:** persona=behavioral-attention, feature=`ratio_last_rm` (implied move at
entry / LAST quarter's realized move), predicted sign "+", registered statistics
tail+ic+median. Discovery data only (`data/discovery_smallcap.csv`, n=2892,
winners=79 (2.7%), 5 season clusters 2024Q2–2025Q2, 2024Q2 has only n=8).

## 1. Mechanism

Recency anchoring after a quiet print. A HIGH implied-vs-last-realized ratio
mostly means the previous earnings move was unusually SMALL, not that the
implied is objectively rich. Anchored expectations (and mechanical
history-based vol marks) price the next event off the quiet print, while true
earnings-move magnitude mean-reverts toward the name's own norm — so the next
print disproportionately produces the violent snap-backs that become big
winners. This cuts AGAINST the naive sell-screen reading of the same column
("rich vs last realized ⇒ sell vol"): if "+" holds on tail, the sell-side lens
is backwards for tail prediction.

Confound identified ex-ante during probing: `ratio_last_rm` is a product of two
things — (a) the implied-richness LEVEL (shared with `ratio_avg_rm`) and (b)
the RECENCY component (last move small vs the name's own average). The
harness-legal derived feature `ratio_last_rm/ratio_avg_rm` = (im/last_rm)/(im/avg_rm)
= **avg_rm/last_rm** cancels the implied level and isolates (b). `ratio_avg_rm`
alone is the no-recency control.

## 3. Committed position

The evidence adds up to: **the recency mechanism is real for tail prediction,
but the registered raw feature conflates it with implied richness, and the
richness part points the other way.** Raw `ratio_last_rm` splits into level ×
recency, and the probes decompose cleanly: (i) the no-recency control
`ratio_avg_rm` shows ZERO tail effect (X0037, −0.3pp, p=0.60), so richness
per se does not make winners; (ii) the pure recency measure `avg_rm/last_rm`
shows the STRONGEST tail effect (X0034, +2.04pp on a 2.7% base ≈ winner rate
roughly 3.7% vs 1.7%, t=3.26) AND a positive median-split net-return diff
(X0039, +1.65pp, t=3.23) with flat rank-IC (X0036) — tail-shaped, not a
monotone rank signal; (iii) raw `ratio_last_rm` inherits a diluted tail effect
(X0011, +1.49pp, t=4.11) but its ic and median legs come back decisively
NEGATIVE (X0018 IC=−0.16; X0027 median −6.9pp) because the richness level
drags typical outcomes down even as the recency component adds tail winners.
So the sell-side lens on this column is indeed backwards for tail prediction —
the high half loses a little more often and still produces materially more
big winners — but the registered "+ on all three statistics" form is wrong:
only tail survives on the raw column.

- **Position:** Trades where the last realized earnings move was smaller than
  the name's own average realized move (`ratio_last_rm/ratio_avg_rm` above its
  cross-sectional median, i.e., avg_rm/last_rm > ~1) carry a big-winner
  (net ≥ +50%) rate about 2.0pp above the below-median half — roughly a
  doubling of the 2.7% base rate — plus a +1.6pp median net-return edge;
  raw `ratio_last_rm` should be used ONLY as a tail screen (never as a rank
  signal, IC is −0.16) and preferably in the level-cancelled form.
- **Confidence:** medium (~65%). Three sign-correct probes at p≈floor with an
  internally consistent decomposition and a clean control null, but: only 5
  (effectively 4) season clusters, so the bootstrap-t is likely optimistic;
  +2pp on n=689 is on the order of ~13 excess winners; and all three
  significant p's sit at the 1/4000 bootstrap floor, which the auditor should
  stress with season-drop jackknife.
- **Boundary conditions:** small-cap discovery panel, 2024Q3–2025Q2, and only
  the subsample with realized-move history (n=1340 of 2892 for the raw column;
  n=689 for the pure ratio, which needs ≥2 prior prints — a selection toward
  older/covered names). Says nothing about history-free names. The claim is
  tail-only for the raw column; the ic/median "+" legs of the registered
  hypothesis are refuted there.
- **What would flip this:** (1) season-drop jackknife showing the X0034 tail
  diff is carried entirely by 2024Q3 (the 4.1%-winner-rate season) — that
  would demote to FRAGILE/regime artifact; (2) shuffled-label calibration
  showing the 5-cluster bootstrap-t rejects far above nominal — that would
  void the p's and leave the effect below the honest floor; (3) sign flip of
  the avg_rm/last_rm tail split on liquid historical.
- **Evidence weight:** 4 of 7 probes support (X0011, X0034, X0039 significant
  at |t| ≥ 3.2 with the predicted sign; X0036 flat-IC is consistent with the
  tail-not-rank reading); 2 refute the registered all-statistic form (X0018,
  X0027, |t| ≥ 3.2 opposite sign); 1 control null (X0037) that sharpens the
  mechanism. Effect sizes: tail +1.5pp (raw) to +2.0pp (pure) on a 2.7% base.

## 4. Verdict

**SUPPORTED** — tail target only, with mechanism refinement: the effect is the
recency component (best feature form `ratio_last_rm/ratio_avg_rm`, i.e.,
avg_rm/last_rm), at +2.0pp tail-rate lift on a 2.7% base (t=3.26, MBF 0.005),
recency-specific (control `ratio_avg_rm` null at −0.3pp). The ic and median
legs of the registered raw-column prediction are REFUTED (both significant
with the opposite sign). Raw p's only — family-wise standing awaits BH-FDR
over the full registry, and the 5-cluster bootstrap caveat stands until the
finding-auditor's jackknife.
