**Data:** discovery_smallcap.csv (sha b80d380463d7c8f9), n=2892 events, 79
winners (long_ret_net >= +0.50, base rate 2.7%), 5 seasons (2024Q2–2025Q2).

**USABLE-N FIRST (skeptic flag from HYPOTHESES.yaml, addressed up front):**
the H11 ratio requires prior implied-move history in BOTH components; the
harness reports usable **n = 474 of 2892 (16.4%)** on every H11 ratio probe

conclusion below is conditional on the 474-event implied-history

subpopulation is NOT neutral — see section 3.

## 1. Mechanism

ratio_avg_im = im_entry/avg(prior implied moves) and ratio_avg_rm =
im_entry/avg(prior realized moves), so their ratio cancels im_entry and
equals avg(prior realized)/avg(prior implied) — the name's own historical
realized-vs-implied gap, fully ex-ante, a pure per-name property with no
current-pricing content. Names that chronically MOVE more than they IMPLY
are structurally under-reserved by vol sellers, so big winners (net >= +50%)
and better rank outcomes should concentrate in the high half (sign "+").

## 3. Committed position

The evidence adds up to a real rank-channel effect whose STRENGTH does not
come from the property H11 was built to isolate. The registered ic leg is
strong and correctly signed (+0.153 at the perm p-floor 0.0005, X0054), the
median variant agrees (+4.7pp net, p=0.016, X0056), and the registered TAIL
leg — the actual top-decile claim — is null (+0.84pp, p=0.716, X0051)
against a detectable floor of ~4.7pp implied by the harness bootstrap SE
(0.844pp/0.36 ≈ 2.3pp, t=2 needs ~4.7pp) at n=474. Two skeptic results
reframe the ic win. First, coverage is itself informative: n_prior_im alone
rank-predicts net return on the FULL panel (+0.133 at the perm floor,
X0062), so the 474-event subsample is a favorably selected population and
part of any within-subsample rank effect may be depth-of-history loading —
the frozen harness cannot stratify within-subsample, so that residual
confound is an open auditor item (n_prior_im does NOT predict the tail,
X0060, so the tail null is not coverage-masked). Second, the decomposition:
the denominator ALONE, ratio_avg_rm — which retains im_entry, i.e. current
cheapness vs the name's own realized history — has a LARGER rank effect
(−0.217, n=695, perm floor, X0066) on 47% wider coverage than the
im-cancelled H11 ratio. Since the H11 ratio is ratio_avg_im/ratio_avg_rm,
its +0.153 is arithmetically the denominator's −0.217 diluted by the
numerator's weaker −0.055 (H1/X0002, same 474-event footprint). The
mechanism's key design choice — cancelling im_entry to get a "pure
structural VRP gap" — DESTROYS signal rather than purifying it: what pays
is current implied cheapness vs realized history, not the historical gap
per se. If anything from this cell is a re-lock candidate, it is
ratio_avg_rm, not the registered H11 ratio.

- **Position:** Above-median avg(prior realized)/avg(prior implied) earns
  higher net return through a distribution-wide rank channel (IC +0.153,
  perm p=0.0005, n=474) but does NOT measurably concentrate +50% winners
  (tail +0.84pp vs a ~4.7pp detectable floor); the signal is a diluted form
  of ratio_avg_rm (IC −0.217, n=695), so the correct object is "im_entry
  below ~0.96x the name's average prior realized move", not the
  im-cancelled historical gap.
- **Confidence:** medium (~60%) on the rank channel being real in this
  subpopulation (perm p at floor, median leg agrees, but the unresolved
  n_prior_im depth confound within the covered subsample caps confidence);
  high (~85%) that the tail-rate effect at n=474 is below ~4.7pp.
- **Boundary conditions:** exists only where n_prior_im >= 1 (16% of
  discovery events — older, longer-optioned names); discovery small-caps
  2024Q2–2025Q2; rank/median channel only, no demonstrated tail
  concentration; the stronger ratio_avg_rm version needs n_prior_rm >= 1
  (24% coverage) and is NOT a registered hypothesis — using it requires a
  disclosed registry re-lock before the lockbox opens.
- **What would flip this:** (a) auditor stratifying the 474-event subsample
  by n_prior_im (or partialling depth out of the ranks) and finding the
  ratio's IC collapses below ~0.05 — that converts SUPPORTED into
  coverage-artifact REFUTED; (b) season-drop jackknife killing the IC when
  2024Q3 (36 of 79 winners) is removed; (c) validation-set IC coming back
  <= 0 or |IC| below the detectable floor at its (even thinner) usable n.
- **Evidence weight:** 3 of 3 registered+variant H11-ratio probes carry the
  predicted sign; 2 of 3 significant at perm p<=0.016 (ic at the 0.0005
  floor); the tail leg is a null at +0.84pp vs ~4.7pp detectable; skeptic
  probes: 1 of 2 (X0062) exposes a live confound, and the decomposition
  probe (X0066) shows a strictly stronger, wider-coverage sibling.

## 4. Verdict

**SUPPORTED** — on the rank-IC channel only, correct pre-registered sign,
perm p=0.0005 at n=474, subject to family BH-FDR and two mandatory auditor
kill-tests (n_prior_im stratification; season-drop jackknife). The
registered TAIL leg is **NULL-WITH-POWER**: any big-winner-rate difference
across the median split of the H11 ratio is, if present, smaller than
~4.7pp at n=474 (harness bootstrap SE ~2.3pp) — we could have seen that; we
saw +0.84pp (p=0.716). Attribution caveat is binding: the effect is
subsumed by unregistered ratio_avg_rm (IC −0.217, n=695, X0066), so the H11
mechanism as written (im-cancellation is the point) is only partially
vindicated.
