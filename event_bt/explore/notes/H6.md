# H6 — prem_frac_entry (microstructure persona, predicted sign "-")

## 1. Mechanism

Absolute-convexity pricing. `prem_frac_entry` is the straddle premium as a
fraction of spot: a low value means the market has marked the name as quiet
in absolute terms. Convexity per premium dollar is largest exactly where the
straddle is cheapest, and vol sellers who quote quiet names off recent
realized behavior systematically under-reserve for regime breaks. If the
mechanism is real, big winners (long_ret_net >= +50%) should concentrate in
the cheap-premium half and rank-IC vs net return should be negative.

Pre-registered confound (flagged at assignment): premium/spot is roughly
proportional to the implied move, which co-moves with ambient vol (H3) and
with the cheap-ratio features (H1). A negative result on the raw feature is
only evidence for a DISTINCT H6 mechanism if it survives normalization by
ambient vol; otherwise it is H3 wearing a premium costume.

## 3. Committed position

The evidence adds up to: a directionally consistent but statistically weak
raw pattern whose sign is inherited from ambient vol, not from a distinct
absolute-convexity channel. All three raw-feature probes carry the predicted
negative sign, but none clears even raw p<0.05 (best: IC -0.133, p=0.065,
MBF 0.19 — "weak" on the Bayes-factor scale, and far from the ~0.012 a lone
BH survivor needs at family size 8). The telling detail is that an IC of
-0.133 at n=2892 would be overwhelming under iid, yet the season-cluster
bootstrap only gives t=-1.82: the negative relationship is season-
concentrated, exactly what an ambient-vol-regime confound produces. The
kill shot is the normalization probe: dividing by iv_amb_entry flips the IC
to +0.034 (sign mismatch, X0032), i.e. essentially all of the raw negative
IC is the ambient-vol component. The one surviving crumb is the normalized
tail stat (-1.37pp, p=0.035, X0035) — but it is below the ~2-3pp tail
detection floor at this n, contradicts its own IC in sign, and the
normalized feature is nearly collinear with H2's im_entry/iv_amb_entry
(where the same tail/IC tension belongs). I take the side: there is no
independent H6 mechanism; whatever prem_frac_entry knows, iv_amb_entry
already said.

- **Position:** prem_frac_entry has no distinct predictive channel — after
  normalizing by ambient vol the residual effect is |IC| ~= 0.03 and tail
  ~= 1.4pp, both below the harness detection floors (|IC| >= ~0.06,
  tail >= ~2-3pp); the raw negative IC of -0.133 is the H3 ambient-vol
  channel expressed through premium.
- **Confidence:** medium (60%). Five probes, internally coherent story, but
  the normalized tail probe (X0035, p=0.035) is a genuine loose end that a
  fuller partial-ranking design could reopen.
- **Boundary conditions:** holds on small-cap discovery data with
  season-cluster inference; says nothing about the liquid cell. Applies to
  the DISTINCT-mechanism claim only — the raw feature may still rank-order
  weakly (as H3's shadow) and could look "predictive" in any pooled
  regression that omits ambient vol.
- **What would flip this:** a within-season or ambient-vol-stratified probe
  where low prem_frac_entry still shows tail-rate lift >= 3pp with p below
  the BH bar; or H3 itself coming back null (then the raw -0.133 IC would
  need a new owner and H6 becomes the leading candidate again.)
- **Evidence weight:** 3 of 3 raw probes sign-consistent but all p >= 0.065;
  1 of 2 normalization probes sign-flipped (the IC, the diagnostic one);
  effect sizes: raw tail -2.3pp, raw IC -0.133 (season-unstable), residual
  IC +0.034, residual tail -1.4pp.

## 4. Verdict

**NULL-WITH-POWER** (as a distinct mechanism). Detectable-effect statement:
at n=2892 with season-cluster bootstrap-t this cell could have detected a
stable tail-rate difference of ~2-3pp or a season-stable |IC| >= ~0.06; the
ambient-vol-normalized residual came in at IC +0.034 / tail -1.4pp, below
those floors. Any true absolute-convexity effect beyond the ambient-vol
channel is smaller than that.

**Reconciliation notes:** (1) The raw-feature results (X0013/X0021/X0030)
should be read jointly with H3 (X0004/X0010) — if H3 is supported, H6 is
redundant, not corroborating; do not count them as two independent hits.
(2) The normalized variant prem_frac_entry/iv_amb_entry is ~collinear with
H2's im_entry/iv_amb_entry; its tail/IC sign tension (X0035 vs X0032)
belongs in the H2 discussion. (3) H1 correlation (cheap ratios) was flagged
but not separately probed here — no extra trials spent on it.
