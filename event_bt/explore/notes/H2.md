# H2 — vol-dealer — im_entry/iv_amb_entry (event-share leverage), predicted sign +

## 1. Mechanism

Ex-ante story: the straddle premium at entry is a blend of the discrete
earnings-event component and ambient (diffusive) vol. When the event
component dominates — implied move large relative to ambient vol — a given
repricing of the implied move should move the position price more, so
high event-share entries convert the pre-earnings repricing channel into
P&L with more leverage. Prediction: big winners (long_ret_net ≥ +50%)
concentrate in the HIGH half of im_entry/iv_amb_entry (sign +).

The mechanism has a hidden assumption worth stating: it treats the size of
the repricing as independent of the entry-time event share. If instead the
event share is itself a price — high share meaning the event is already
fully flagged and priced — the leverage story inverts.

Decomposition reading: the rank-IC of the ratio (−0.179) and of raw
im_entry (−0.181) are nearly identical, so in rank terms the negative
relation is carried largely by the implied-move level itself. But the
TAIL effect is much more stable for the ratio (t=−3.15 vs −1.21 for raw
im_entry under the season-cluster bootstrap): normalizing by ambient vol
removes the regime dependence of the median split and makes the winner-rate
gap robust across seasons. So the ratio is not doing nothing — it is the
regime-robust version of "the event is expensively priced."

## 3. Committed position

The evidence does not merely fail to support H2 — it points the other way
on all three statistics, decisively on the pre-registered tail target
(−1.8pp winner-rate gap, t=−3.15, p=0.00025, MBF 0.007) and clearly on IC
(−0.179, p=0.018). High event-share entries produce FEWER +50% winners,
not more. The leverage mechanism is wrong because it ignores that event
share is itself a price: when the implied move already dominates ambient
vol, the market has fully flagged the event, upward repricing room is
spent, and the long straddle is bought at peak event premium. This is the
mirror of the cheap-vol channel (H1/H6): winners come from underpriced
events, and im/iv_amb high is close to the definition of "not underpriced."
The sign-flipped pattern ("low event share → more winners") is a candidate
NEW hypothesis, but it was obtained by looking — it may not inherit H2's
registration and would need its own registry slot with the full trial
count against it.

- **Position:** Entries with im_entry/iv_amb_entry above the sample median
  have a tail-winner rate ~1.8pp LOWER (on a ~10% base) than below-median
  entries, and event-share ranks negatively with net return (IC ≈ −0.18);
  the ex-ante "+" leverage mechanism is contradicted, and the effect works
  through "event already fully priced," with the ambient-vol normalization
  supplying regime robustness that raw im_entry lacks.
- **Confidence:** high (>80%) that the "+" prediction is wrong; medium
  (50–80%) that the reversed effect is a real, promotable regularity
  (post-hoc sign, one dataset, median target not significant).
- **Boundary conditions:** holds on small/mid-cap discovery events with
  non-missing im_entry (n=2,004; the ~31% missing-im rows are unscored and
  could differ); tail and IC targets only — the median-split return gap is
  directionally consistent but not distinguishable from zero; untested on
  liquid names and on the sealed validation split.
- **What would flip this:** a sealed-validation (or liquid-transfer) run of
  the tail target showing a POSITIVE hi-minus-lo winner-rate gap ≥ +2pp, or
  the discovery tail effect vanishing (|diff| < 1pp) once the finding-
  auditor drops the dominant season(s) — the latter would demote "reversed
  effect real" to artifact, though it would not resurrect the "+" claim.
- **Evidence weight:** 0 of 3 registered-sign probes support "+"; 3 of 3
  carry the opposite sign, 2 of 3 individually significant (tail p=0.00025,
  IC p=0.018); 2 diagnostic variants consistent with the reversal at
  IC ≈ −0.18.

## 4. Verdict

**REFUTED** (for the registered "+" direction). This is not a null: the
detectable floor at this n (~±2pp tail-rate diff, |IC| ≈ 0.06) was
exceeded — in the opposite direction. The reversed-sign pattern is flagged
for the orchestrator as a possible new registry entry (mechanism: event
fully priced when event share is high), to be treated as post-hoc and
audited for season concentration before any promotion.
