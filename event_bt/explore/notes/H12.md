# H12 — dte_entry (microstructure: listing-granularity dilution)

**Cell:** feature `dte_entry`, predicted sign `-`, registered statistics tail+ic.
**Data:** `data/discovery_smallcap.csv` (n=2892, winners 79 = 2.7%, 5 seasons). Discovery only.

## 1. Mechanism

The T-14 entry buys the nearest post-event expiry. Where the expiry grid is
coarse, the position carries extra non-event days: the event variance is a
thinner slice of the straddle premium, so a given event repricing moves the
position price proportionally less, and the larger ambient time-value
component decays over the hold. Prediction: longer dte_entry → worse net
outcomes, fewer +50% winners (sign −). Counter-mechanism (vol-dealer):
coarse-grid names have less-developed options markets and fatter mispricing,
which would flip the sign to +. Skeptic flag: dte also scales theta paid,
partly mechanical through net returns.

**Distribution reality check (describe pass + free descriptive read):**
`dte_entry` is nearly degenerate — 2764/2892 (95.6%) sit at exactly 14;
range 10–18; 56 trades <14 (delayed entries), 72 trades >14 (expiry 1–4
days past the event). There are NO true monthlies (no 30–45 dte) in this
panel. The harness median split is therefore `>14` (n_hi=72) vs `<=14`
(n_lo=2820). The hypothesis as written ("weeklies vs monthlies") is not
testable here; what IS testable is expiry-grid misalignment by a few days.

## 3. Committed position

The evidence adds up to a real, correctly-signed return drag from expiry
misalignment, but NOT (demonstrably) a tail-winner effect. The governing IC
probe is the strongest non-control result available for this cell: Spearman
IC −0.0585 at perm p=0.001 (MBF 0.0045), corroborated by the median split
(−0.0999 net-return median gap, perm p=0.0035) and by boot on both. The
monotone bucket pattern runs through both tails of the distribution (delayed
entries do BETTER, overhanging expiries do WORSE), and the gross-return
descriptive read plus near-flat spreads defuse the pure cost-mechanical
skeptic reading; the dilution signature (low im_entry, high iv_amb_entry in
the long-dte bucket) is exactly the predicted fingerprint. The vol-dealer
counter-mechanism (+) is refuted on sign: every probe came out negative. The
registered tail claim, however, is unprovable at this split: with n_hi=72
and base rate 2.7%, the detectable tail-rate difference is roughly ±4pp —
larger than the base rate itself — and we saw −1.4pp. Scope discipline: the
effect lives in 128/2892 trades (4.4%) spanning dte 10–18; extrapolating to
true monthly-only names (dte 30+) is untested.

- **Position:** trades whose entry expiry overhangs the event by 1+ days
  (dte_entry > 14) carry a median net-return penalty of ~10pp via
  event-share dilution, and the effect is monotone in dte across 10–18;
  it is a drag/screen-out signal, not a tail-winner selector.
- **Confidence:** medium (~65%). Strong, consistent p's on the governing
  statistic with perm and boot agreement, coherent two-sided monotone
  pattern, and a mechanism fingerprint in the covariates — but the
  effective sample is only ~128 off-tie trades, one panel, and the long-dte
  bucket is calendar-concentrated.
- **Boundary conditions:** holds for the T-14 small-cap pipeline where dte
  variation is 10–18 days from grid misalignment; says nothing about true
  monthly-only listings (absent from panel), nothing about the +50% tail
  target (underpowered), and the effect should shrink wherever event share
  (im_entry/iv_amb_entry) is controlled — it may be H2's mechanism wearing
  a calendar costume rather than an independent signal.
- **What would flip this:** (a) an auditor season-drop jackknife where
  removing 2025Q2 (32 of 72 long-dte trades) kills the IC sign or pushes
  perm p above ~0.1; (b) a gross-return ranking test (not in the frozen
  harness — auditor must construct from logged columns) showing the IC is
  absent on gross, which would reinstate the theta/cost-mechanical reading;
  (c) the IC flipping positive on the liquid historical cell, which would
  favor the vol-dealer counter-mechanism.
- **Evidence weight:** 4 of 5 probes support at |IC|=0.059 / median gap
  −0.10 (perm p=0.001 and 0.0035); the 1 non-supporting probe (tail) is a
  power failure, not a contradiction (observed −1.4pp, floor ~±4pp).

## 4. Verdict

**SUPPORTED** (on the ic + median channel, sign −, perm-governed) —
with the registered tail component explicitly **NULL-WITH-POWER**: at the
degenerate 72-vs-2820 split, a tail-rate effect, if any, is below the
detectable floor of ~±4pp winner-rate difference (observed −1.4pp,
p=0.733). Not registry-locked (iteration 2); any promotion requires a
disclosed registry re-lock and an auditor pass on the season-jackknife and
gross-ranking kill-tests named above.
