# H1 — vol-dealer / ratio_avg_im (predicted sign "-")

**Data:** discovery_smallcap.csv (sha b80d380463d7c8f9), n=2892 events, 79 winners
(long_ret_net >= +50%, base rate 2.7%), 5 seasons (2024Q2–2025Q2).
**Feature coverage caveat:** ratio_avg_im is non-null for only n=474 events
(names with prior implied-move history); variant ratio_last_im covers n=1106.
Every H1 conclusion is conditional on that subpopulation.

## 1. Mechanism

Cheapness vs the name's OWN implied history: when the entry implied move is far
below the average of the name's prior event implied moves (ratio_avg_im << 1),
dealers/sellers have marked the event down relative to its demonstrated norm.
That leaves the most room for the implied move to be bid back up into the
event, so the largest long-vol repricings — and hence the tail winners — should
concentrate in the low-ratio half. Prediction: above-median ratio_avg_im has
FEWER winners, lower rank-IC vs net return, lower median net return (sign "-"
on all three registered statistics).

## 3. Committed position

The evidence adds up to a real but MIS-SPECIFIED effect: the cheapness-vs-own-
history direction is right — all 6 of 6 probes carry the predicted negative
sign, and the rank-wide channel is strong (avg-ratio IC -0.055 at the bootstrap
p-floor 0.00026 with t=-9.68, i.e., stably negative under every season-cluster
recomposition; last-ratio median-split -3.8pp of net return, also at the
p-floor) — but the SPECIFIC registered claim, that TAIL winners concentrate in
the low-ratio half, is not what the data show. The tail-rate legs are tiny in
both variants (-0.84pp p=0.15; -0.54pp p=0.70) against harness-implied
detectable floors of roughly ±1.5pp (n=474) and ±2pp (n=1106). Low ratio buys
you a better whole-distribution outcome (rank/median), not demonstrably a
higher big-winner rate. Auditor flags: (a) only 5 season clusters — the
bootstrap-t of -9.68 measures cross-season-composition stability and the floor
p=1/4000 plus MBF~0 overstate precision; (b) the effect lives in the 16-38%
of events that have prior-IM history, a selected (older/optioned-longer)
subpopulation.

- **Position:** Entries with ratio_avg_im below ~0.99 (the subsample median)
  earn higher net returns through a distribution-wide repricing channel
  (IC ~ -0.05), but the incremental big-winner rate in that half is under
  ~1.5 percentage points — the tail-concentration version of H1 is not
  supported at this n.
- **Confidence:** medium (~65%) on the rank/median channel (2 of 6 probes at
  the p-floor, 6/6 sign agreement, but only 5 clusters and one family not yet
  BH-corrected); high (~85%) that the tail-rate effect is below ~1.5-2pp here.
- **Boundary conditions:** holds only where the feature exists (n_prior_im>0;
  474 of 2892 events for avg, 1106 for last); discovery small-caps 2024Q2-
  2025Q2 only; strongest legs differ by variant (IC for avg, median for last),
  so the exact functional form is not pinned down.
- **What would flip this:** IC probe on the sealed validation set coming back
  positive or |IC| < 0.02 (harness-detectable floor there); or the auditor's
  season-drop jackknife showing the negative IC vanishes when any single
  season (esp. 2024Q3, which holds 36 of 79 winners) is removed.
- **Evidence weight:** 6 of 6 probes support the sign; 2 of 6 significant at
  the bootstrap p-floor (X0002 IC -0.055, X0033 median -3.8pp); the 2 tail
  probes are nulls at effect sizes below their ~1.5-2pp detectable floors.

## 4. Verdict

**SUPPORTED** — on the rank-IC/median (repricing) channel, with correct
pre-registered sign; subject to family BH-FDR and auditor kill-tests.
The tail leg specifically is NULL-WITH-POWER: any big-winner-rate difference
across the ratio_avg_im median split is, if present, smaller than ~1.5pp
(n=474; harness bootstrap SE ~0.6pp) and smaller than ~2pp for ratio_last_im
(n=1106; SE ~0.95pp) — we could have seen that; we saw -0.8pp and -0.5pp.
