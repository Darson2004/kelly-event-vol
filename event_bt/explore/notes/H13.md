# H13 — n_prior_rm (realized-move history depth), predicted sign −, tail

**Persona:** behavioral-attention. **Data:** discovery_smallcap.csv (n=2892, 5 seasons, 1014 tickers, winner rate 2.7%). **Iteration 2.**

## 1. Mechanism

Ex-ante story: `n_prior_rm` counts how many prior realized earnings moves the
pipeline has for the name — a proxy for how much "memory" the market has of
the name's event behavior. Young option histories mean pricing with less
memory and fatter two-sided mispricing, so big winners (long_ret_net ≥ +0.50)
should concentrate in the LOW half.

**Mechanism problem found during the probe (not just the pre-registered
skeptic flag):** in this panel `n_prior_rm` spans only 0–4 and increments by
roughly one per season for the 914 recurring tickers (914 of 1014 tickers
appear in >1 season). It therefore measures *panel tenure* — how many seasons
into the dataset the event falls — not the name's actual option-market
maturity. A name that has traded options for 15 years and a genuinely young
name both get n_prior_rm = 0 in 2024Q3. The intended mechanism is not
measurable with this column as built.

## 3. Season-balance analysis of the median split (mandatory check — FAILED)

Descriptive crosstab (counts only, no statistics computed outside the
harness). Global median of n_prior_rm = 1.0, so high half = {2,3,4}:

| season | low half | high half | % high | per-season median n_prior_rm | winner rate (describe) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024Q2 | 8 | 0 | 0.0% | 0 | 0.0% |
| 2024Q3 | 880 | 0 | 0.0% | 0 | 4.1% |
| 2024Q4 | 923 | 3 | 0.3% | 1 | 2.8% |
| 2025Q1 | 88 | 571 | 86.6% | 2 | 1.1% |
| 2025Q2 | 29 | 390 | 93.1% | 3 | 2.4% |

The split is essentially a 2024-vs-2025 calendar split (split sizes 1928/964,
not 50/50, because the feature is integer-valued with heavy ties at 0 and 1).
Per the pre-registered skeptic flag, this makes the raw tail statistic
**uninterpretable regardless of p**. The X0049 raw stat of −1.76pp (high half
1.6% vs low half ~3.3% winner rate) is exactly the vintage effect visible in
the describe pass: 2024Q3 ran 4.1% winners while 2025Q1 ran 1.1%. The
boot-vs-perm gap on the identical statistic (p 0.113 boot vs 0.483 perm,
X0058 vs X0049) is the confound showing up in the machinery: once season is
conditioned on, the "effect" evaporates.

Power consequence: within-season permutation of the median-split tail stat
has discriminating power only where the split varies inside a season —
2024Q4 (3 high), 2025Q1 (88 low), 2025Q2 (29 low), i.e. ~120
minority-bucket events. The IC probe (X0052) is the higher-power admissible
read because it uses the full 0–4 ordinal values within each season.

## 4. Committed position

The evidence adds up to: nothing here, and the one number that superficially
supports H13 is a documented artifact. The only sign-matching probe (X0049
tail, −1.76pp) rides a median split that is a near-perfect calendar split, and
its within-season permutation p is 0.48 — conditional on season the split
carries no information. The two finer-grained admissible reads both lean the
WRONG way (ic +0.046 p=0.127 X0052; median +0.034 p=0.544 X0055): if anything,
*more* history weakly associates with better ranked returns within season,
the opposite of the registered mechanism, though neither reaches
significance. On top of the empirical null, the feature cannot measure the
intended construct — it is a 0–4 panel-tenure counter, not option-history
depth — so even a positive result here would not have supported the
"young names are mispriced" story. Do not re-register this cell; if the
mechanism is worth testing at all it needs an external listing-age or
options-history-length column, not a pipeline-internal counter.

- **Position:** n_prior_rm has no within-season predictive content for
  big-winner membership; its raw tail-stat sign-match is entirely a
  2024-vs-2025 vintage artifact (2024Q3 4.1% vs 2025Q1 1.1% winner rates
  loaded onto a calendar-degenerate median split), and the feature as built
  measures panel tenure (0–4), not market memory, so the registered
  mechanism is untestable in this panel.
- **Confidence:** medium (50–80%) that the within-season effect is truly
  ≈ zero; high (>80%) that the cell is not promotable as registered, because
  the split-degeneracy finding is structural, not statistical.
- **Boundary conditions:** holds for this 5-season small-cap discovery panel
  where n_prior_rm ⊂ {0..4} and correlates ~1:1 with season. Says nothing
  about true option-history age (IPO cohorts, recently-listed-options names)
  measured from data external to the panel; that hypothesis is untested, not
  refuted. Also says nothing about liquid names.
- **What would flip this:** (a) a genuine listing-age / options-history
  feature (not panel tenure) showing a sign-correct within-season perm
  p < 0.05 on tail; or (b) with more panel seasons breaking the
  feature-season collinearity, a within-season tail perm p < 0.05 with the
  low half over-representing winners. A wrong-signed IC firming up
  (perm p < 0.05 positive) would flip me from NULL to REFUTED.
- **Evidence weight:** 0 of 3 admissible perm probes support the hypothesis
  (X0049 sign-correct but p=0.48 on an uninterpretable split; X0052 and
  X0055 wrong-signed at p=0.13/0.54); 1 comparison probe (X0058 boot)
  documents the confound. Effect sizes all small: |tail diff| 1.8pp
  (artifact), |rank-IC| 0.046 (wrong sign).

## 5. Verdict

**NULL-WITH-POWER** (with a structural-confound flag: the registered tail
statistic is uninterpretable as specified). Detectable-effect statement: on
the admissible within-season reads at this n, we could have seen a rank-IC of
roughly |ρ| ≈ 0.09 (the X0052 read of 0.046 sat at p=0.127), and — because
median-split variation inside seasons is confined to ~120 minority-bucket
events — only a within-season tail-rate difference on the order of a
doubling of the 2.7% base rate. We saw less than both, and the ranked reads
leaned opposite to the prediction.
