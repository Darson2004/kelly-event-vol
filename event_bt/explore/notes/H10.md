# H10 — skeptic — amb_rv_hold (LEAKY POSITIVE CONTROL)

**THIS CELL EXISTS TO BE EXCLUDED.** `amb_rv_hold` is realized volatility
computed OVER THE HOLD WINDOW — it mechanically overlaps the outcome
(`long_ret_net` is realized over that same window). It is registered as a
positive control only: (a) to prove the frozen harness has power to detect a
real effect in this data, and (b) to calibrate the statistical signature of a
mechanically contaminated "signal" so genuine candidates can be compared
against it. It is **never promotable to trading**, never enters the
BH-FDR family as a candidate, and must appear in the final report only in
the controls/exclusions section.

## 1. Mechanism

Not an economic mechanism — an accounting identity. A long-premium
earnings-event trade nets ≥ +50% only if the underlying moves a lot during
the hold; realized vol over the hold IS a monotone transform of "moved a
lot." So P(winner | high amb_rv_hold) must exceed P(winner | low
amb_rv_hold) by construction, regardless of any ex-ante information content.
The prediction is directionless in the underlying: high hold-window RV
accompanies both big winners and big losers, so the contamination should be
strong in the tail-membership statistic but weak-to-absent in
direction-sensitive statistics (rank-IC vs signed net return, median split
of signed net return). That asymmetry is itself the calibration target.

## 2. Probes run

Data: `data/discovery_smallcap.csv` (DISCOVERY, sha b80d380463d7c8f9),
n=2,845 usable (2,892 rows, base winner rate 2.7% = 79/2,892, 5 season
clusters). All stats from frozen `eval_harness.py`; all runs logged to
`experiments.jsonl`.

| X0007 | amb_rv_hold | tail (Δ winner-rate, hi−lo median split) | +0.03447 | 3.21 | 0.0075 | 0.0058 | yes |
| X0015 | amb_rv_hold | ic (Spearman vs long_ret_net) | +0.01135 | 0.21 | 0.7535 | 0.9792 | yes |

Context (descriptive, from `eval_harness.py describe`, not inferential):
winner-median amb_rv_hold 0.624 vs rest-median 0.428. The +3.45pp tail-rate

winner rate of the low-RV half.

## 3. Committed position

The control fired exactly where it had to and exactly where it should not
have, and that dual result is worth more than a blanket "it worked." On the
registered statistic (tail), the leak is unmistakable: t=3.21, p=0.0075,
MBF=0.006 with season-cluster bootstrap-t — so the harness demonstrably has
power to flag a real tail-rate effect of ~+3.5pp on a 2.7% base at n≈2,845,
which retires the worry that a genuine candidate could exist but be
invisible to this machinery at effects of that size. Equally important, the
IC and median probes came back dead flat (ρ=0.011, Δmedian=0.004, both
p≈0.75, MBF≈0.98): hold-window RV is magnitude information, symmetric across
both tails, so it cannot rank signed returns. That gives us the
contamination fingerprint: **tail fires strongly while IC and median stay
null**. Any genuine candidate showing that exact pattern should be
interrogated for outcome overlap or magnitude leakage before being believed
as a directional signal (though for a long-premium book, an *ex-ante*
"will move a lot" feature with this pattern could still be legitimate — the
distinction is entirely whether the feature is computable before entry,
which amb_rv_hold is not).

- **Position:** amb_rv_hold shows the expected mechanical leak — tail-rate
  gap +3.45pp (p=0.0075, MBF=0.006) with null IC/median — confirming harness
  power at the ~+3.5pp/2.7%-base scale and establishing "tail-only
  significance with flat direction stats" as the contamination signature;
  the cell is a control and is permanently excluded from promotion.
- **Confidence:** high (>80%). The effect is required by construction, the
  registered statistic detected it at MBF 0.006, and the null side probes
  agree with the mechanism's symmetry prediction.
- **Boundary conditions:** holds on discovery small-caps 2024Q2–2025Q2 with
  the +50% winner threshold; the power statement covers tail effects
  ≥ ~+3.5pp at n≈2,845 — it does NOT establish power for smaller effects,
  for the liquid cell (n≈193), or for IC-channel effects (the flat IC here
  is a property of this symmetric feature, not evidence about IC power).
  The fingerprint heuristic applies to magnitude-type features; a genuine
  skewness/direction feature would not be expected to mimic it.
- **What would flip this:** the tail probe failing to fire (p > 0.10) on a
  re-run or on the validation split — that would indicate the harness lacks
  power or the pipeline is broken, and every other cell's null verdicts
  would become uninterpretable until diagnosed. A strongly positive IC
  (ρ > 0.1) would also flip the symmetry story and suggest the outcome
  variable itself is skew-contaminated.
- **Evidence weight:** 3 of 3 probes match the mechanism's predictions
  (1 strong positive where required, at +3.45pp / t=3.21; 2 nulls where
  symmetry predicts nulls, at |effect| ≤ 0.011).

## 4. Verdict

**SUPPORTED** (as positive control — SUPPORTED-AS-CONTROL, **never
promotable**, excluded from the candidate family by construction). The
harness has power: it detected the mandatory leak at p=0.0075 / MBF=0.006 on
the registered tail statistic. Calibration deliverable: mechanically
contaminated magnitude features present as tail-significant with flat
IC/median; genuine candidates matching that fingerprint require an
outcome-overlap audit before promotion.
