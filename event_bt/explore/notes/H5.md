# H5 — microstructure: spread_frac_entry, predicted sign "−"

## 1. Mechanism

Registered dual reading. (a) **Mechanical cost:** winners are defined on
`long_ret_net`, and the bid-ask spread is a direct subtraction inside that
number — high `spread_frac_entry` trades pay more to get in and out, so any
"-"-signed association with net returns is partly bookkeeping, not
prediction. (b) **Illiquidity signal:** wide spreads proxy for neglected,
thinly-made names; the signal reading of the registered "−" sign says such
names produce *fewer* big net winners ex-ante. The registry itself carries
the skeptic flag: nothing here is promotable unless it survives a
gross-return ranking. This note's job is to separate (a) from (b)
quantitatively, since the harness targets are all net-based.

Magnitude prior (from the brief): median entry spread ≈ 29% of premium; a
full round trip at phi=0.5 costs ≈ 15% of premium at the median, far more in
the wide-spread half — against WIN_THR = +50% this is big enough to
reclassify only *marginal* winners, not deep ones.

## 3. Committed position

The three graded numbers look like the strongest result in the family
(IC −0.49, median −25.6pp, both p=0.00025), and they are almost entirely
**accounting, not prediction**. The realized cost differential between
spread halves is ~31pp of premium — larger than the whole −25.6pp median
net-return gap, and the hi-spread half actually has the *better* gross
median (+3.3pp). So the IC/median legs are the harness re-discovering that
net = gross − cost with cost ∝ spread. On the decision-relevant statistic —
big-winner membership — entry spread does nothing detectable (−0.76pp,
p=0.16). And the null itself is informative: if the two halves had *equal*
gross tails, the ~31pp extra cost in the hi half should have deleted the
gross-winner band [+50%, ~+80%] (~3.6pp of trades in the lo half), predicting
a net tail diff of roughly −3 to −4pp. We see −0.76pp because the hi-spread
half generates ~3x more gross tail winners (+10.9pp), and cost eats almost
exactly that surplus. Both readings of the registered "−" sign therefore
fail: pure-cost predicts a *bigger* negative tail diff than observed, and
the illiquidity-signal reading has the *wrong sign on gross* (wide-spread
names have fatter gross tails, not thinner — though mid-to-mid marking
overstates attainable gross in wide-spread names, so +10.9pp is an upper
bound on any true effect). H5 must not be promoted on its IC/median
p-values; per its own registered skeptic flag it dies on the gross ranking.

- **Position:** `spread_frac_entry`'s huge net-return association (IC −0.49)
  is ≥ ~90% mechanical cost (31pp cost differential vs 26pp median gap, with
  gross gap +3pp the wrong way); as an ex-ante big-winner signal it is dead —
  net tail effect −0.76pp against a ±2pp detectable floor, and the gross
  tail effect is opposite-signed (+10.9pp descriptive).
- **Confidence:** high (>80%) that the IC/median legs are mechanical and
  that no "−"-signed tail signal ≥ 2pp exists; medium (50–80%) on the
  "+"-signed gross-tail reading being real economics rather than a
  mid-marking artifact (descriptive only, never graded).
- **Boundary conditions:** holds for small-cap discovery events 2024Q2–2025Q2
  at phi=0.5 execution and WIN_THR=+50%; does not speak to the liquid
  universe (spreads there are a far smaller premium fraction, so the
  mechanical channel shrinks and any residual signal could look different);
  the exit-spread variant (X0038, −1.45pp, p=0.034 raw) is nominally
  significant but is the *most* cost-contaminated variant (202 cost-killed
  winners concentrate exactly where exit spread is paid) and will not
  survive BH q=0.10 at family size ~8 unless p≲0.012.
- **What would flip this:** a harness-graded gross-return ranking (would
  require an amendment) showing tail diff on gross ≤ 0 for hi-spread — that
  would kill the "cost cancels an opposite gross effect" decomposition; or a
  fill-price-marked gross showing the +10.9pp gross tail surplus vanishes,
  which would collapse the story to pure noise-in-marking; or a net tail
  diff beyond ±2pp appearing in the sealed validation set.
- **Evidence weight:** 4 of 4 probes match the registered "−" sign, but 2 of
  4 (ic, median) are cost-identity artifacts at effect sizes (−0.49,
  −25.6pp) fully covered by the 31pp measured cost differential; the 1
  decision-relevant probe (tail) is null at −0.76pp with a ±2pp floor; the
  variant (exit tail, −1.45pp) is marginal and maximally contaminated.

## 4. Verdict

**REFUTED** (as a promotable illiquidity signal for big winners — the graded
IC/median effects are mechanical net-cost accounting and the gross-side
direction contradicts the registered "−" sign), with the tail leg stated as
null-with-power: any true net tail-rate effect of entry spread, if it
exists, is **below the ±2pp detectable floor at n=2892** (we observed
−0.76pp, p=0.16, against a 2.7% base winner rate).

Auditor guidance: do NOT promote on X0017/X0028 p-values; the registered
skeptic flag (gross-ranking survival) is the binding test and it fails
descriptively. If any spread-family probe is taken to Stage 4 it should be
the tail statistic only, and the ±2pp floor makes that a waste of a
family slot.
