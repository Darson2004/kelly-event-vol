# H9 — skeptic / spot_entry (NULL CONTROL)

## 1. Mechanism

None, deliberately. Share-price level has no ex-ante economic reason to
predict which pre-earnings straddles become big winners. H9 exists to
detect grid-wide artifacts: the registered alarm was "if winners load on
LOW-priced stocks, the persona hypotheses are penny-stock vol artifacts."
Expected (hoped-for) verdict: NULL-WITH-POWER.

What actually surfaced is a *different* mechanical channel than the one
registered: `long_ret_net` embeds transaction costs, and fractional
option-spread drag is much larger for low-priced underlyings. So price
level "predicts" the *level* of net returns (high spot = less cost bleed)
without predicting anything about gross outcomes or tail membership.

## 3. Committed position

The null control did NOT come back clean, but it fired in a diagnostic,
not alarming-for-the-tail-question, way. The three probes split cleanly
by target: tail membership (the actual research question) shows nothing
(+0.6pp, t=0.68, vs a detectable floor of roughly ±2pp at this n), while
ic and median both fire (p=0.02 / 0.034, MBF 0.063 / 0.113) with HIGHER
price predicting BETTER net returns — the *opposite* direction of a
penny-stock vol artifact. The descriptive decomposition pins the pathway:
the gross-return IC is +0.024 (dead), the net-return IC is +0.146, and
low-priced names bleed a median 19.1pp to costs vs 14.9pp for high-priced
names. This is a transaction-cost gradient wearing a signal costume, and
it means every NET-return ic/median statistic in this grid has a
cost-drag component loaded on price level. Contamination ranking by
|rho(spot, X)|: **H6 (prem_frac_entry, −0.436)** is most exposed — its
predicted "−" sign points the SAME way as the cost artifact, so any H6
ic/median support must be audited against gross returns before belief;
**H3 (iv_amb_entry, −0.268)** likewise has its predicted sign aligned
with the artifact on ic/median (tail is its clean read); **H5
(spread_frac_entry, −0.153)** IS the cost variable — its registered
skeptic flag is hereby confirmed as live, not hypothetical; **H10**
(−0.420) is already the leaky control; **H4** (month, −0.145) registered
tail-only, which is the clean channel. **H1, H2, H7 are clean of
price-level loading (|rho| ≤ 0.012).**

- **Position:** spot_entry carries no tail-membership signal (effect, if
  any, < ~2pp at n=2892), but its net-return IC of +0.146 is a real,
  mechanical transaction-cost artifact (gross IC +0.024) that
  contaminates ic/median statistics of any feature with |rho(spot)| ≳
  0.15 — specifically H6, H3, H5 — in the direction those hypotheses
  predicted.
- **Confidence:** high (>80%) that the ic/median effect is cost-mechanics
  rather than alpha (two agreeing harness stats + gross/net decomposition
  that collapses the effect at the source); high that the tail channel is
  null at the stated floor.
- **Boundary conditions:** holds for NET-return grading on this
  small-cap panel (5 seasons, spot p10–p90 = $5.24–$85). Does not apply
  to gross-return grading or to the tail statistic (winners at +50%
  clear the cost hurdle either way). Only 5 season clusters, so
  bootstrap-t is coarse.
- **What would flip this:** (a) an auditor-run gross-return ranking
  showing spot_entry IC comparable to +0.146 — would mean price level is
  real alpha, not costs, and a much louder alarm; (b) season-drop
  jackknife killing the ic/median effect — would downgrade to
  noise-fired control; (c) any re-cut showing winners loading ≥2pp on
  the LOW-price half — would re-raise the original penny-stock alarm.
- **Evidence weight:** 2 of 3 probes show the net-level effect (IC
  +0.146 p=0.020; median +6.25pp p=0.034); 1 of 3 (tail, +0.62pp,
  p=0.503) is null with power. Descriptive decomposition (non-graded)
  supports the cost mechanism unambiguously.

## 4. Verdict

**REFUTED** (as a clean null control) — split by target:

- **tail: NULL-WITH-POWER.** Detectable floor ~±2pp tail-rate diff at
  n=2892 / 2.7% base rate; observed +0.62pp (t=0.68). Big-winner
  membership does not load on price level in either direction.
- **ic / median: control FIRED** (p=0.020, 0.034; MBF 0.063, 0.113), and
  the effect is a transaction-cost artifact, not alpha (gross IC +0.024).

Consequence for the grid: treat the TAIL statistic as the clean channel.
Demand a gross-return audit before believing any ic/median result for
H6, H3, or H5 (and note H10's overlap). H1, H2, H7 are unexposed.
