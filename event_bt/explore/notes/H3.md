## 1. Mechanism

Complacency mispricing: when the ambient (non-event) vol regime is quiet at
entry, the whole option surface — including the earnings event premium — is
anchored low. Sellers extrapolate the calm; the event's true surprise
variance is not regime-dependent to the same degree, so straddles bought
into low-ambient-vol conditions carry systematically underpriced tails that
violent prints then expose. Prediction: feature-above-median (high ambient
IV at entry) half does WORSE, i.e., negative statistic on all targets.

Registered TENSION (ex-ante): the contaminated liquid descriptive cell
showed big winners clustering in HIGH-vol quarters, so the regime-macro
persona disagreed with itself. A positive-sign result here would have been
a real dialectical outcome, not an error.

## 3. Committed position

The evidence adds up to a real but CENTRAL-TENDENCY effect, not (yet) a
top-decile tail effect. All three targets land on the predicted negative
sign — the small-cap panel comes down on the complacency side of the
registered tension, against the liquid high-vol-quarter clustering. The
median-split test is the strongest single result in the cell (p=0.012 raw,
MBF 0.045 — decisive-ish odds against chance even before family
correction, and coincidentally sitting exactly at the BH lone-survivor
threshold p≈0.012 for family size 8). The rank-IC (-0.070) just clears the
|IC|≥0.06 detectable-floor magnitude but is only marginal under season
clustering (p=0.104). The tail probe — the actual research question — is
directionally right (-1.2pp on a 2.7% winner base rate, i.e. roughly
2.1% vs 3.3% between halves) but statistically nothing (p=0.44) once the
5-season cluster structure is respected; winner rates swing 1.1%–4.1%
across seasons and 2024Q3 alone holds 36 of 79 winners, so the tail
channel simply cannot be resolved at this clustering. The descriptive
contrast (winners: LOW ambient IV at entry 0.458, HIGH realized ambient
vol during hold 0.624) both fits the mechanism and dissolves the tension:
the liquid cell's "winners in high-vol quarters" is plausibly a
contemporaneous/realized-vol fact, not an entry-time ambient-IV fact —
quiet entry, violent aftermath are the same trade seen at two timestamps.

- **Position:** Entries made below the panel-median ambient IV (~0.52)
  earn ~6pp better median net long-straddle returns (median-split stat
  -0.062, p=0.012) via the complacency-repricing channel; the big-winner
  (>=+50% net) tail enrichment, if any, is below the detectable floor at
  this season-cluster structure.
- **Confidence:** medium (~55-60%) that the negative-sign central-tendency
  effect is real; low (<40%) that it specifically enriches the top-decile
  tail.
- **Boundary conditions:** holds as a median/rank effect on small-cap
  discovery, 2024Q3–2025Q2; NOT established for the tail target; NOT
  established on liquid names (where descriptive evidence points the
  other way for quarter-level clustering); vulnerable to 2024Q3
  concentration.
- **What would flip this:** median-split sign flip (or p>0.5) on the
  sealed validation set; or an auditor season-drop jackknife showing the
  X0019 median effect vanishes when 2024Q3 is removed; or the gross-return
  variant failing (if the effect is a spread-cost artifact — low-ambient-vol
  names may simply have tighter spreads, cf. H5 skeptic flag).
- **Evidence weight:** 3 of 3 probes sign-match; effect sizes: tail
  -1.2pp (n.s.), IC -0.070 (marginal), median -0.062 (p=0.012, MBF 0.045).

## 4. Verdict

**SUPPORTED** (weak — carried by the median/IC channels, not the tail).

Tail-target rider (NULL-WITH-POWER for that sub-claim): with 5 season
clusters and a 2.7% winner base rate, the harness could have detected a
half-split tail-rate difference of roughly ±2pp only under favorable
clustering; we observed -1.2pp with p=0.44 — the tail effect, if any, is
below that detectable floor on this panel. Promotion to the registry
should ride on the median/IC statistic, with the H5-style gross-return
audit attached before any validation spend.
